Stage 03 is a single vaulted chamber: an underground reservoir whose piers rise from the flooded basin to carry the vault overhead, with the route threading between them. You descend from the sluice, choose the flooded floor or the high gallery, and climb back out at the valve. Built entirely from already-accepted assets — the seamless damp platform kit, the unlit vault pier, the hanging lantern and the six-pose Cistern Lurker. Falling costs the climb back up, not a life: the basin is the floor of the chamber and it catches you. The water hazard returns when there is a painted water surface to show it.
