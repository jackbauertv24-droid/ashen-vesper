A SMALL ENCOUNTER, A COMPLETE LOOP

Read the road. Learn the rhythm.

A first gameplay prototype built with retained artwork. Enemy poses and background masks are provisional; the interaction rules are playable.

01

Find your footing

The camera follows across five screens. Jump the broken sections and climb the optional upper routes. Compare camera and air-control settings above.

02

Watch the pilgrim

A gold warning precedes each staff strike. Step away, then punish its recovery. Three hits defeat a guardian. You have five health points.

03

Bring an ember home

Swing on the ground or in the air. Strike a hanging brazier and collect its falling ember. Defeat the final guardian and press E at the gate. The sanctuary restores health and saves your restart location.
